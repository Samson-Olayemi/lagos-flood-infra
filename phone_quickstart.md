# Phone Quick Start: how to record a site

For the pilot (3-4 Oct 2026). Read `docs/field_protocol.md` for the full rules. This page is the short version.

## What you need

| Item | Why | Can the phone do it? |
|---|---|---|
| Phone (GPS, camera, form app) | Position, photos, typing answers | Yes |
| **Measuring tape (3-5 m) and a stick about 1 m long** | Road width, drain width and depth, pothole size | **No. A tape is needed.** |
| Power bank | Phone runs all day | - |
| Pen and paper | Backup form (Appendix A of the protocol) | - |

The PRD lists the tape as required. Without it you can only pace the road width (method `pacing`) and estimate by eye
(method `visual_estimate`). Both are allowed in the form but are weaker data. Drain depth cannot be measured without a tape or stick.

## One-time setup (do this before 2 Oct)

**You have an iPhone.** KoboCollect is Android-only, so on iPhone you use the **Kobo web form in Safari**.

1. On your **laptop**, make a free KoboToolbox account at kobotoolbox.org.
2. New project, then upload `configs/field_form_v1_0.xlsx` as an XLSForm, then Deploy.
3. On the project page open "Collect data" and copy the web form link that says online-offline. Open it in **Safari on the iPhone**, while you have internet. Add it to your Home Screen.
4. **Photos are not taken inside the form.** Use the normal Camera app (see the protocol, section 8). First set Settings, Camera, Formats, **Most Compatible**.
5. **Airplane-mode test (important):** open the form, switch on airplane mode, fill a whole dummy form, save, switch airplane mode off, send. Check it arrives in your Kobo account. Web-form offline mode on iPhone is only partly reliable, so we need to see it work.
6. **If offline fails:** fill the paper backup form (Appendix A of the protocol) in the field and type it into the same web form on the laptop in the evening.

## At a site (about 10 steps)

1. Check it is safe. If not, skip it.
2. Open a new form. Type the Site ID. Pick the stratum (`PILOT` for pilot sites).
3. Stand in the middle of the 50 m stretch of road. Tap to record the GPS and wait for a good accuracy number.
4. Type LGA, area and road name.
5. Road: surface type, width, potholes, cracking, rutting, edge.
6. Drain (right-hand side): type, width, depth, blockage, sediment, plants, waste, condition, flow, outlet. No drain? Choose `none`.
7. Flood signs: standing water, water marks, debris line, overtopping, erosion, pavement damage.
8. Photos in the Camera app, marker card first, then 7 photos (protocol section 8). Enter the photo count in the form.
9. If you cannot record something, the form asks why. Always pick a reason. Never skip silently.
10. Save. Move on.

## Every evening

- Send the finished forms (when you have internet).
- Copy the photos to a laptop or drive. Do not delete them from the phone yet.
- Tick the site off in the tracker.

## Never

- Never enter a drain, flowing water, floodwater or a traffic lane.
- Never commit photos or raw field data to GitHub.
